# Finance

Seven projects spanning valuation, forensic accounting, forecasting, capital budgeting and
portfolio management. The valuations and models ship with their underlying Excel. The valuations ship with their underlying Excel models.

| Project | Method | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [TechnoGlobal forensic accounting](technoglobal-forensic-accounting/) | US GAAP review, restatement | 8 red flags; net income overstated $141M; covenant breach uncovered |
| [Amazon (AMZN) equity research](amazon-investment-proposal/) | DCF / FCFF, 33-sheet model | BUY, $292.99 target, +29.5% |
| [Coca-Cola (KO) equity analysis](coca-cola-equity-analysis/) | Comparables + DDM + sentiment | Fairly valued, modest upside |
| [BigTech quarterly analysis](bigtech-quarterly-analysis/) | Three-statement analysis, cohort metrics | Nine quarters of financials, churn, and FY2024 projections |
| [Portfolio risk management](portfolio-risk-management/) | Portfolio management, hedging | Three holdings exposed to one geopolitical shock in opposite directions, each with a specific defensive instrument |
| [Walmart sales forecasting](walmart-sales-forecasting/) | Time-series forecasting | Seven forecasting methods compared across 12 sheets, feeding forecasted financial statements |
| [Best Buy capital budgeting](best-buy-capital-budgeting/) | NPV, IRR, PI, payback | Full investment-criteria set on a WACC-derived discount rate, plus 10-K and hedging analysis |

## Skills

**Valuation** — DCF and FCFF modelling, WACC and CAPM, dividend discount models, comparable
company analysis, multiples (P/E, P/S, P/B, EV/EBITDA), terminal value, scenario and sensitivity
analysis

**Financial reporting** — US GAAP application (ASC 606, 326, 330, 250, 360, 842, 985-20),
financial statement restatement, ratio and covenant analysis, earnings-management detection

**Analysis** — bottom-up segment forecasting, peer benchmarking, reconciling conflicting
valuation methods, model-limitation assessment

## What these three have in common

Each one ends up arguing with its own output. The Amazon DCF is paired with a bear case that
prices the thesis failing. The Coca-Cola DDM returns $180–190 against a $70 market price, and the
write-up explains why that result should be discarded rather than banked. The TechnoGlobal
restatement turns on noticing that eight individually defensible adjustments all move the same
direction.

A valuation that only produces a number isn't finished. The useful part is knowing where it
breaks.
