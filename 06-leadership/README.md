## [Club finance →](club-finance/)

Two of these roles came with a budget, and these are the workbooks I ran them on.

**[HULT FC budget, 2024–25 and 2025–26](club-finance/hult-fc-budget-24-26.xlsx)** — estimated
versus actual across the season, tracking league fees (Dalton spring and fall), the Beanpot and
fall tournaments, membership income, amounts owed to Hult, and event funding requests, plus the
Volta League game schedule.

**[Boston Arab Society finances](club-finance/boston-arab-society-finances.xlsx)** — a budget
tracker, income tracker and bank reconciliation against the society's BofA statement, with
per-event P&L across five events: the Tasty Event, football, puppy yoga, Eid, and padel.

Running estimated-versus-actual against a real bank statement, per event, is the same exercise
as a small business close — just with a student society's money, which is harder to get back
when it is spent badly.
