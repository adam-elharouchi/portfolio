# Layers — Brand Strategy Live Challenge

**Brand positioning · audience strategy · channel planning · risk mitigation**

> Live Challenge Phase 3 final report, Hult International Business School.
> Team 5: Adam El Harouchi, Youssef Chama, Nacer Senhaji, Justin Nikondeha, Alexandre Folliet.

## Problem
A live client challenge for Layers, a guided self-reflection product. The brief: find the
positioning and go-to-market approach that gets people to start.

## Core insight
**People want to grow, but most do not know how to begin self-reflection.**

The barrier isn't motivation or awareness — it's that the first step is undefined. That reframes
the marketing problem completely: the job is not to convince anyone that self-reflection is
valuable, it's to make starting concrete enough to act on.

## Strategy
Position Layers as the **simplest and most emotionally resonant first step** into guided
self-reflection, through content that triggers recognition and curiosity rather than persuasion.

**Creative concept — "Moments You Cannot Ignore."** Real emotional moments leading to one simple
invitation: *start with one conversation.* Recognition does the persuading; the call to action
only has to be small enough to say yes to.

## Deliverable structure
1. Core insights and strategy
2. Target audience and messaging
3. Channels and execution
4. Risks and mitigation
5. Open questions for the client

Carrying a risks-and-mitigation section and an explicit list of open questions back to the client
is what separates a proposal from a pitch — it states where the plan could fail and what the team
still needs to know.
