# Small Pizza Chain — Strategic Vision and Market Expansion

**Business strategy · growth strategy · market entry**

> Business Strategy midterm essay, Hult International Business School. Sole author.

## Problem
Set a strategic direction and expansion path for a small pizza chain — the classic question of
whether a local operator with a working formula should deepen its position or extend its
footprint, and on what basis.

## Approach
A structured strategy essay working from the firm's existing competitive position through to a
defensible growth plan: where the current advantage actually comes from, which expansion routes
are consistent with it, and what would have to be true for each to work.

## Skills
Strategic vision setting · competitive positioning · market expansion and entry strategy ·
growth strategy · strategic analysis frameworks
